ESA Pest and Disease Web Application and AgroApp

Problem. Sugarcane pests and diseases in Eswatini had to be inspected and monitored consistently across commercial fields, nurseries and research fields.

What I built. The ESA Pest and Disease Web Application for inspecting commercial production fields, with a Nursery Module for nursery fields. Also AgroApp, which the Research Division uses to inspect and monitor research fields and to evaluate and test sugarcane varieties.

Impact. Pest and disease occurrences are identified, monitored and managed from one system. 50 field officers captured 1,000+ inspection surveys.

Impact. Live on Google Play, so businesses keep selling through outages.
